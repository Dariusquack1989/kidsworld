# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Task catalogue with 40 templates, own tasks for several children, categories, age groups and repeat options.
- Week plan per child: base time, TV start and end, extra time and screen-free days.
- Copy a day to other days, or a week to another child.
- Paired devices are listed and can be renamed or removed one by one.
- The phone page can be added to the phone's home screen.
- The device list shows each phone's last IP address.
- FAQ for common problems.

## 1.0.0

First version as a project of its own.

- Four animated worlds with a mascot.
- Child profiles with allowed apps, favourites and free apps.
- Tasks that earn screen time, with parent confirmation.
- Parent PIN, daily time and bedtime.
- Guard through Android's accessibility service.
- Phone page on the home network, with pairing.
- English and German.
