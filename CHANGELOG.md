# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (not released yet)

First version as a project of its own.

- Four animated worlds with a mascot.
- Child profiles with allowed apps, favourites and free apps.
- Tasks that earn screen time, with parent confirmation.
- Parent PIN, daily time and bedtime.
- Guard through Android's accessibility service.
- Phone page on the home network, with pairing.
- English and German.
